I set myself a goal: build 100 apps and put every one on camera.

A few apps in, it stopped being a hobby and became a project. Projects need managing. So before the next app, I built the tool to run it.

This is the 100 Apps tracker. Built in a day, and it's app #6 on its own board.

What it does:
• One square per app, color-coded from idea to building to shipped to video published
• Click a square for start and ship dates, hours by week, costs, and a dated note every time I learn something
• Drag a square to rearrange. Everything moves with it
• Costs and weekly hours roll up on their own
• Dropped projects stay on the board with a slash through them. Killing a bad idea on purpose is a decision, not a failure
• The whole dataset exports to one file, so anyone can check my math

It replaced the spreadsheet I would have used a year ago. Same data, but now the decisions are built in: what's next, what it cost, whether I'm on pace.

The skills are the ones I built in factory automation: scope the work, track actuals against the plan, count the cost, report straight, adjust.

7 apps started, 2 shipped, 1 with a video up, 95 hours logged. Target: 100 by March 31.
