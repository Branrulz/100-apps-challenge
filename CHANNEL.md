## Channel description

**Short (header):**
Laid off from factory automation. Building 100 apps on camera to see if a blue collar guy can make it as an app builder. Honest numbers, every time.

**Full (About page):**
I spent my career in factory automation: PLCs, controls, keeping production lines running. Then I lost the job.

So I'm making a bet. 100 apps, built by me, every one of them on this channel. Real apps, in the store, that people can download. I'll show what I built, how I built it, what broke, and what it earned. Good or bad, the numbers go on screen.

I'm not a programmer by training. I'm a guy who fixed machines and figured things out. If you're blue collar and wondering whether you could do this too, watch and find out with me.

New app episodes as fast as I can ship them. Six apps down before the channel even started.

Progress tracker and every app: github.com/Branrulz/100-apps-challenge

**Keywords:** blue collar to code, 100 apps challenge, learn to code, app development, indie apps, career change, laid off, factory automation, PLC, self taught developer
